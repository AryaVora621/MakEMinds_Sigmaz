# t01: What happens to balls on a HIVE tip (BIOBUZZ 2026-27)

Lead: hive-physics. Worker: t01 (Haiku). Read-only research. No code or other docs edited.

## Answer (short)

The manual does not say that POLLEN or NECTAR fall out of, spill from, or leave a CELL when a HIVE tips. It says a CELL "can hold" pieces, and it only scores pieces left in the **upward-facing** CELL at match end. So "stay" is supported by implication, not by an explicit rule. "Fall out" has no source in the manual. Whether pieces in the previously-up CELL stay put, roll, or are removed during a tip is **UNVERIFIED** against the manual.

## Exact manual wording

**Section 9.6 HIVE Structure (p.69):**
- "Each CELL is a three-dimensional structure that can hold NECTAR and POLLEN."
- "Each HIVE is on a pivot and can tip so that one of the CELLS is facing upwards at any given time. Each HIVE is bi-stable and will hold its position until enough POLLEN or NECTAR are LAUNCHED into the upwards-facing CELL."

**Section 9.6.2 HIVE and CELL (p.70):**
- "The opening of the CELL is approximately 20 in. (50.8 cm) wide by 14 in (35.6 cm) tall and 12 in. (30.5 cm) deep as shown in Figure 9-11."

**Section 10.3.1 SCORING ELEMENTS (p.82-83):**
- "Each HIVE is tilted such that one CELL is pointed down, and the other CELL is pointed up, as shown."
- Initial NECTAR: "3 NECTAR in each upward-facing CELL of corresponding color (6)" and "NECTAR in the CELL is placed contacting the back wall of the CELL."

**Section 10.1 MATCH Overview (p.82):**
- "Each time a HIVE is TIPPED, an ALLIANCE is allowed to enter one of five NECTAR initially staged in the ALLIANCE AREA."

**Section 10.5.1 HIVE Scoring Criteria (p.87):**
- "The HIVE is considered TIPPED when: A. it moves from one stable state to the other stable state with the downwards-facing CELL becoming the upwards-facing CELL, and B. subsequently, the damper on the HIVE that was previously not contacting the frame begins to contact the frame."
- "LAUNCHING at the downward-facing CELL while a HIVE is tipping may disrupt its movement and may result in the TIP not being achieved."
- "LAUNCHING into the upward-facing CELL is the only allowed way to earn a HIVE TIP. ROBOTS must follow G417 and cannot disrupt or cause a HIVE TIP in other ways."
- "POLLEN and NECTAR remaining in CELL: At the end of the MATCH, any POLLEN and/or NECTAR left in an upward-facing CELL will earn points for that ALLIANCE."

**Section 10.1 and 10.5 timing (p.86):**
- "Assessment of POLLEN and NECTAR remaining in the CELL will occur after all SCORING ELEMENTS and ROBOTS have come to rest at the conclusion of the MATCH."

**Exit wording (only one found, not a HIVE rule):**
- "POLLEN that exits the FIELD will be reintroduced into the FIELD at the earliest safe opportunity by FIELD STAFF" (section number not confirmed from the extract; line 605 of the section 10 text).

## Interpretation

- **Stay in the CELL:** Supported by "can hold NECTAR and POLLEN" and by end-of-match scoring of pieces in the upward CELL. This is the most likely reading, but the manual does not say it directly.
- **Fall out or spill on tip:** No rule found. The manual does not mention a drop, spill, or ejection on tip.
- **Pieces in the previously-up CELL after a tip:** Not addressed. The manual scores only the upward CELL at match end, so older pieces in the downward CELL are not scored at the end. That is an inference, not a stated rule.
- **Pieces that are launched while the HIVE is moving:** The manual warns that launching at the downward CELL "may disrupt its movement." It says nothing about balls falling out.
