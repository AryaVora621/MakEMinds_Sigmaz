# t11: BallWorld hive capture then tip (headless reproduction)

Owner: Haiku worker, lead hive-physics. Method only; no repo files edited except this report.

## What I ran
- Throwaway `node --input-type=module` script on stdin (not saved). Read-only use of `web/js/balls.js`, `hivecycle.js`, `hivemesh.js`, `web/data/scene.json`, `colliders.json`, `hive_collider.bin`.
- Setup copied from `tests_js/sim_physics.test.mjs` (real hive mesh via `parseHiveCollider`) and `tests_js/ballkinds_e2e.test.mjs` (`HiveDynamics` + `HIVE_PARAMS`, `onCapture -> dyn.addScoredBall`).
- Red hive at CAD pose `audience_up` (angle -30 deg). Up cell = `audience`, aim `[59.267, 55.517, 58.313]`, opening normal `[-0, -0.866, 0.5]`. Hive pivot `[59.25, 72, 43.95]`.
- Tick = `dyn.red.step(DT); dyn.blue.step(DT); w.setHiveAngles(...); w.step(DT)`, DT = 1/60. The caller drives the tip. BallWorld does not tip itself.
- Drops: one pollen at a time, from `[aim_x + ox, aim_y + n_y*2 + oy, aim_z + 14]` (z about 72.3), zero velocity. Offsets cycled over 11 values. Each ball run until free-and-sleeping, or about 6 s.
- Ran twice. Run 1 used 20 drops and stopped at 8 captures. Run 2 repeated the same drops and snapshotted every ball before each tick to catch the tip frame.

## Capture results (run 2 numbers match run 1)
- Drops: 20. Captures: 8 (drops 0, 1, 7, 8, 11, 12, 18, 19 in the 0-based index). Captures happened at x 51 to 67 and y about 53 to 58.
- Tip started on the frame of the 8th pollen count (`tipProgress` reached 1). `dyn.red.onTip` fired once, `-> far_up`. Hive angle went -30 to +30 over 0.6 s.
- Failed drops (12) landed free on the floor at z = 1.39 or on top of earlier balls (z = 4.16). Drops at y of about 45.8 and 49.8 (outside the opening) never captured.
- HiveDynamics `scored` list reads 11 after the tip: 8 pollen plus 3 starting nectar. `reset()` seeds those 3 nectar with `kind: nectar` in the up cell. BallWorld has no matching balls (`w.reset([])`), so the 3 nectar are invisible. They do not count toward the tip.

## Ghost check (the question asked)
- Scored balls in the cell that went down (`audience`) did NOT stay at their pre-tip world positions. They moved with the hive.
- Rotation is rigid about the pivot. Hand check for `shot1`: pre-tip `(51.251, 62.472, 49.924)`, relative to pivot `dy = -9.528`, `dz = 5.974`. Rotating +60 deg gives `y' = -9.937 -> 62.063`, `z' = -5.264 -> 38.686`. Measured end state `(51.251, 62.062, 38.686)`. Match.
- Displacement for the 7 scored balls at the tip frame: `dz = -11.238` (5 balls), `dz = -14.858` (2 balls), `dy = -0.41`. Distances 11.25 and 14.86 in. This is the expected rigid move, not a ghost.
- Mechanism: `balls.js` stores scored balls in hive-local coords (`_lx/_ly/_lz`). `step()` calls `_placeScored(b, hv)` each frame with `hv.delta`, so they ride the hive. `releaseOnTip` is false (default), so BallWorld also keeps them in the down cell (no removal on tip).

## Free balls near the hive during the tip
- `shot6` was sleeping on the hive at `(59.59, 78.79, 39.66)`. After the tip it rose to z = 47.0 at 0.5 s, fell to z = 19.6 at 1.0 s, and came to rest at z = 1.39 by 2.0 s. It was pushed by the rotating hive surface, not left floating.
- `shot17` behaved the same way (sleeping at z = 41.05, moved to 70.09 on the floor by 2 s).
- Post-tip state after 5 s: all 8 scored balls still `scored` at the same positions; free balls all asleep on the floor (z = 1.39) or at z = 4.16 on stacks.
