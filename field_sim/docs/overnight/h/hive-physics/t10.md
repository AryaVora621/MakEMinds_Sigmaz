## Answers
- Ball in the down cell after tip: YES, all 11 (releaseOnTip=false). None after releaseOnTip=true because they were removed.
- Does any ball leave on its own: NO. The model has no physics: a tip only flips state and clears counts.
  Balls only leave when releaseOnTip=true, and then all of the down cell leaves at once, not just the tipped balls.
- "Stuck" reading: with the default, down-cell balls are never re-evaluated, so they stay in the down cell for
  the rest of the match. They do not drop out. That matches the header rule, but it is the reported symptom.
