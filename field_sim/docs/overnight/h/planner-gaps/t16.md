## 2. Jargon and over-long text
- Line 680 (toolsHtml hint, about 200 chars): "Click the field to append a waypoint with Bezier handles (Shift: straight). Click a path to insert one. Drag anchors, handles and the rotation dot. Right-click or Delete removes." Shows only when Editor Settings is open, but it is the only on-canvas help. Split into one short line.
- Line 737 (constraints note): "Preview only. On the robot, speed and braking come from PathConstraints in your Constants." Jargon. Shorten to "Preview only. Robot limits: TODO (PathConstraints)."
- Line 801 (optimizer placeholder): "Not available in this simulator: there is no trajectory optimizer, the preview uses the Pedro follower or the built-in trapezoid model." Long and only informs. Delete the section.
- Line 793 (towards placeholder): "Not supported yet: the Pedro export has no point-towards behaviour." Delete the section.
- Line 746 (final state note): "The last path uses tangent heading; change its heading mode in Waypoints to edit this." Keep, shorten.
- Line 779 (sampler badge): "Sampler: ... Samples come from the real Pedro Pathing 2.1.0 core" in a title. Jargon for drivers. Move to Export tooltip.
- Labels: "Bezier handles", "Tangent", "Constraint zone", "in/s2", "Pedro Visualizer .pp", "Copy Pedro 2.1.0 Java", "t 0.25 to 0.75", "Reversed (drive backwards)" is fine. "Rotation snap" (line 685) and "Grid lines" are fine.
- Tooltip TIPS.addzone (line 73, about 110 chars) and TIPS.addrot (line 73) are long for a tooltip.

## 3. Icon-only buttons and tooltips
- The btn() helper (line 656) always sets title and aria-label from TIPS[id] or the label. Every icon id used in pathui.js has a TIPS entry (checked: undo, redo, mirror, cbadd, eye, lock, up, down, del, split, addctl, delseg, toggle, stop, clear, copy, download, save, load, exportpp, importpp, pnew, pdup, pdel, select, curve, line, delete, addev, addrot, addzone, noop). Result: no icon-only control in pathui.js is missing a tooltip.
- Gap: the "+" in every section header (line 664) uses an aria-label, but the header toggle (line 666, class pp-sec-t) has no title. It has text, so this is minor.
- Gap: the tool buttons (line 679) are icon-only with TIPS text. The tool palette is hidden inside Editor Settings (see 5).
- Gap: pp-tick (line 691) are title-only `<i>` elements with pointer-events:auto. They are not buttons, but they are the only way to see event positions on the slider. Make them focusable or give them a key-based alternative.

## 5. Problems found
1. Tool palette is hidden. Select, Curve, Line and Delete live only in "Editor Settings", which is closed by default (line 802). The keyboard shortcuts (V, L, C) are the only fast path. Move the four tools to the top bar (line 675) and keep snap settings in Settings.
2. Two add buttons for events. The section header "+" (addev, line 791) and the custom row "+" (cbadd, line 772) both add an event. Keep one.
3. Placeholder cards. "Point Towards Zones" (793) and "Path Optimizer" (801) are dead cards. They take two accordion headers and show a "+" that does nothing (noop). Delete both.
4. Starting State and Final State (795-796) are two closed headers with 3 fields each. Merge into one "Start and end" section.
5. Global Constraints and Constraint Zones (798-799) are two closed headers for one concept (speed limits). Merge, with zones as a sub-list.
6. Waypoint row buttons (ins, del) are always visible on every row. Show them on hover or focus, as the pp-wp-a pattern at planner.css:81-82 already does for path rows. This removes 2 controls per row (up to 8 in a 3-path route).
7. Time readout appears twice: time chip in top bar (line 675) and "x / y s" in the playback bar (line 695). Keep one.
8. Export and playback duplicate autoui.js: Save JSON and Load JSON are in pathui exportHtml (line 777) and autoui export (autoui.js 720-721). Mirror, undo, redo are in both pathui topHtml (676) and autoui headBox (563). Transport (play, stop, step, speed) is in pathui playbackHtml (689-697) and autoui timeline (autoui.js 750-752). When the path editor is hosted in the auto planner (pp-float), hide pathui's playback bar and its Save/Load pair.
9. Delete appears in three places: pp-topbar trash (autoui.js 492), pathui "Clear path" (777), and the Delete tool. Keep the trash icon in the top bar; rename "Clear path" to "Clear all points" to avoid confusion.
10. Settings contains 8 controls and one long hint. Keep Snap as a select; move Magnet, Grid lines and Rotation snap into a closed "Snap" disclosure.

## 6. Proposed removals and merges (priority order)
1. Delete sections towards (793) and optimizer (801) and their TIPS.noop. Saves 2 headers and 2 placeholder cards.
2. Move the tool palette (TOOLS, line 77) from Settings into topHtml (line 675). Reason: primary action, currently hidden.
3. Remove the custom-row "+" (line 772) or the header "+" (line 791) for events. Keep one.
4. Merge startstate + endstate into one "Start and end" section (795-796). Saves one header.
5. Merge constraints + zones into one "Speed limits" section (798-799).
6. Hide the per-row mini buttons until hover or focus (planner.css:104-106 pattern). Resize remaining controls to 32 px (see 4).
7. Shorten the hint (line 680) to: "Click to add. Shift: curve. Right-click deletes."
8. Remove the duplicate time readout (line 695) and duplicate Save/Load JSON (line 777) when hosted in autoui.
9. Keep waypoints as the only default-open section (no change).
