## What the code does (web/js/flowerarm.js:78-95)
- Gate: `arm.progress >= minProgress (0.9)`, tip z in [minTipZ 1, maxTipZ 19].
- Per flower: `dist` = XY distance tip to `opening_center` (z ignored). Reject if `dist > alignTolIn (4)`.
- Alignment: `al = |angDiff(bearing(pose centre -> oc), pose.h + (sign<0 ? pi : 0))|` in degrees, reject if `al > alignTolDeg (20)`.
- Best = smallest `dist`. `angDiff` wraps correctly at +-pi (verified).

## Answers to the audit questions
1. Front and rear bearing/heading math: correct. Rear arm uses h + pi and the x sign flip; sweep confirms.
2. +-pi wraparound: correct (angDiff). No failure found.
3. alignTolIn semantics: XY radius only, z ignored. Opening z = 21.4 in, but feed tip z is 1.5-1.9 in at the gate, so a ~19.5 in vertical gap is never checked. Physically this may be intended (paddle sweeps the stack) but is UNVERIFIED against CAD.
4. Alignment is measured from robot CENTRE, not the tip. Heading error dominates. Because the 4 in position check binds first, the 20 deg tolerance is effectively not used. Consider measuring from the tip or dropping alignTolDeg to avoid implying a looser tolerance.
5. Two flowers near: not reachable with current openings (101.8 in spacing). Code picks min distance, so it is safe if data changes.
6. Opening at wall level: NO. Opening centre is 3.93 in from the wall edge, 21.4 in high. A robot with its front face against the wall (front face on inner wall face at 142.67, centre 133.67) puts the tip 8.3 in past the opening. The working window is a centre distance of 11-18.5 in, i.e. front bumper about 4.6 to 12.1 in from the inner wall face (computed from ROBOT halfX 9, Assumed forward = x).

## Recommendations (not applied; owners decide)
- Planner/autos: do not target a front-against-wall pose. Target centre distance 14-15 in from opening centre along the wall normal (tip ~0.3 in off at d = 15).
- Consider measuring alignment from tip (or documenting that alignTolDeg is secondary).
- Confirm arm angle/hinge/paddle numbers (flower_arm.json says "placeholder until CAD") before trusting the 11-18.5 in window; it moves with hinge.x, arm.length and the deployedDeg.
