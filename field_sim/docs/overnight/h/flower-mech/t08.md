## Answer
Yes, in the modelled physics. A ball released by `feedFromFlower` reaches the front intake mouth and is captured by the roller logic, with no contact with the flower back plate, the robot box, or the floor before capture. This holds for all 4 flowers, at dt 1/120 and 1/60, with the full 4-ball stack. Two gaps remain: the paddle is not in the physics world, and the intake and flower constants are placeholders.

## Held-full case (4 fake entries pre-filled into `world.held`)
- Nothing is captured. Each ball passes the mouth, touches the floor (ball bottom at z 1.39 to 1.49), and comes to rest about 2 in in front of the robot at (46-50, 135.7-138.5). This is the expected hold-limit behaviour.
- The 3 balls released in this case are not recaptured within the 3 s run. Pull radius is 2.5 in, so a ball resting in front of the mouth could be taken once a slot frees (not tested).

## Alignment gate (findFlowerContact, not capture)
- Flower contact (flower 1, arm deployed) with robot lateral offset `lat` (in) and heading offset `deg`:
  - lat 0-1 with 0 or 8 deg: contact.
  - lat 2, deg 0: contact (dist 2.0). lat 2, deg 8: none.
  - lat 3, deg 0: contact (dist 3.0). lat 3, deg 8: none.
  - lat 4, deg 0: contact (dist 4.0, at the limit).
- So the combined offset rule (alignTolIn 4 in, alignTolDeg 20) rejects some poses before the feed starts. The feed never starts, so no ball is lost in flight. Result is not a capture failure.

## Unverified / limits
- The paddle (the arm's tip plate, about 8 x 3.2 in) is not in the physics world. The first flight segment starts at the tip (lx about 14.7 in), so the ball passes through the paddle's volume. Whether it clears the paddle in the real mechanism is not checked.
- `intakeMouth` {x 10.5, z 3, halfY 8.5} and `INTAKE` (pullRadius 2.5, maxLift 3.2, captureGap 0.6) are placeholders or ASSUMED values with no source (`web/data/flower_arm.json` prov = placeholder; balls.js INTAKE has no source). The capture result depends on them.
- The flower is a box placeholder (colliders.json), trimmed to a back plate. The real flower CAD geometry is not checked.
- The existing test (tests_js/flowerarm.test.mjs, "feed works on every flower and balls reach the mouth") only checks that balls enter `ballsInBox`. It does not run capture (no robot or intake set). The new capture coverage is in this sim, not in the test suite.
- Hives are omitted, so ball-hive collisions are not covered. Not relevant at the flower positions tested.
