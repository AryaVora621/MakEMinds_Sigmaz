# t06: BallWorld hive-entrance scoring audit (read-only)

Scope: web/js/balls.js, web/js/hivemesh.js, tests_js/shotaudit.test.mjs. Method: code reading only. No node tests or probes were run.

Answer: Yes. Balls can be scored without a clean crossing of the opening, through three paths: the rim-grace bypass, the start-ball rest rule, and the sleep/strip gap. Two counting problems also exist.

## Scoring rules as implemented
- A free ball is captured in `_hiveCapture` (balls.js:757) when its centre is inside a cell sensor (`insideCell`, hivemesh.js:232) AND either:
  - `b._enter` is set for that cell (set by `crossesOpening` or `crossesGrownOpening` on a front-to-back plane crossing, balls.js:736), or
  - `b.start && slow` for 0.15 s (balls.js:767).
- Sensor is behind the opening plane by OPEN_INSET = 1.0 in and inset 0.5 in from floor, back, lid and sides (hivemesh.js:203, 204-231). A ball on top of the lid, or on the outside, is never inside the sensor.
- `_hiveCapture` returns at once if `b.sleeping` (balls.js:758).

## Findings

1. Rim-grace bypass disables all hive collision for the whole substep (not just the rim). Severity: medium, design-intended but unbounded.
   - `_entering` (balls.js:703-711) is true when the centre is moving toward the opening plane (dQ < dP), is within [-3, 2r+1] of the plane (ENTRY_DEPTH, balls.js:96), and its projection is inside the polygon grown by r (balls.js:707).
   - When true, the mesh sweep bounce is skipped (balls.js:724-726) and the discrete push-out is skipped (balls.js:748).
   - The test uses only the endpoint q, not the swept segment. A ball clipping the solid side plate or floor within r of the mouth edge, moving toward the plane, passes through solid geometry.
   - `crossesGrownOpening` (balls.js:99-107, used at balls.js:736) then sets `_enter` if the centre crosses the plane within r of the polygon, so the ball is scored.
   - Not verified: how deep such a ball can penetrate in one substep. This needs a probe (scripted ball grazing the outer face of a side plate at 0.5 r and 1.0 r offsets, check whether it scores and its depth into the mesh).

2. Balls entering from behind or below use the same bypass. Severity: low.
   - The `dQ < dP` test (balls.js:706) has no sign-of-approach check from the front. A ball 0 to 3 in behind the plane that moves further back still satisfies `_entering`.
   - Floor-level balls under the mouth (projection within r of polygon edge) can pass through the floor for the same reason. Unverified, needs a probe.

3. Start-ball rest rule scores preloads with no opening crossing. Severity: medium, depends on rules intent (unconfirmed).
   - balls.js:767 captures a start ball (`b.start`, set at balls.js:260) after 0.15 s at under 30 in/s inside any cell sensor. No opening crossing is needed.
   - Start balls are created sleeping (balls.js:259). Hive motion wakes them (`_wakeNear`, balls.js:434-435 and 484-488), so tipping the hive can wake and score a preload that was not flagged to score.
   - The capture still increments the hive count through `_pending` and `recordHiveBall` (balls.js:466, 469). It does not increment the driver score because `ball.shot` is false (app.js:434-436).
   - Lead decision needed: should preloads ever score by resting? The docs in balls.js:17-18 say capture requires crossing or rest, so this matches the code but may not match the rules.

4. Sleeping balls in the opening strip are never captured. Severity: medium, uncounted ball. Unverified, needs a probe.
   - A shot ball that crosses the mouth (balls.js:736, `_enter` set) and lands on the floor just behind the plane, within the 1 in OPEN_INSET strip, is outside `insideCell`.
   - Resting contact sleeps the ball after 0.25 s (balls.js:537). Once asleep, `_hiveCapture` returns at balls.js:758, so the ball is never scored unless ball contact or hive motion wakes it (balls.js:603, 619, 674, 687).
   - The shotaudit "stuck" test (tests_js/shotaudit.test.mjs, second test) checks only `insideCell`, so it misses this strip case.

5. Cell recycling makes scored counts go down. Severity: low to medium.
   - When a cell is full, `_capture` splices the oldest scored ball out of `this.balls` (balls.js:412-414).
   - `_captureInfo` totals (balls.js:402) and `stats()` scoredRed/scoredBlue (balls.js:355-356) count only balls still in `this.balls`, so both fall after a recycle.
   - `HIVE_BALL_COUNTS` (balls.js:129-133, 466) is monotone and is not affected. The app currently reads only ball, alliance and cell (app.js:433-436), so the user-visible effect today is limited to `stats()` and the info totals.

6. Balls resting on top of the hive are not captured. Confirmed by reading.
   - The lid is at the sensor top with inset 0.5 (hivemesh.js:204-231), and a ball on the lid has its centre above it. Normal contact also sets `_c` and sleep without capture.

7. Balls grazing the outside of the rim without approaching the plane are bounced. Confirmed by reading.
   - `_entering` requires dQ < dP and the grown polygon. A ball moving along the outside of the rim is bounced by the mesh (balls.js:726 and 748).

## Test gaps (tests_js/shotaudit.test.mjs)
- Test 1, "every counted ball crossed an opening": the assertion at the `for (const c of caps)` loop is a tautology. It asserts `state==='scored' || state==='free'`, which is always true for a counted ball. It does not check crossing.
- Test 3, "no back-door scoring": it checks only that the ball was at any point on the front side (aim-point normal) of the plane. It does not check that the ball passed through the polygon, so a path around the rim passes it.
- Test 2, "no stuck balls": uses `insideCell` only, so it misses the 1 in strip (finding 4).
- No test covers finding 1 (deep penetration through the rim bypass).

## Suggested probes (not run)
- Scripted ball paths at 0.5 r, 1.0 r and 1.5 r offsets outside each side plate and the floor near the mouth. Record whether `_enter` is set, whether the ball is scored, and its maximum depth inside the mesh.
- Drop a shot ball onto the cell floor just behind the mouth. Check whether it sleeps, is unscored, and is scored after a nudge.
- Fill a cell past capacity and compare `stats().scoredRed` before and after the recycle.
