## Coverage gaps, ranked by risk

1. **Tunnelling through the hive at realistic shot speeds (HIGH).** The t12 sweep fails at 200-534 in/s. The "very fast ball" test in shotaudit.test.mjs:98 asserts only `Number.isFinite(caps)`, which is vacuous. The no-tunnel tests in sim_physics.test.mjs use 800-1800 in/s against the perimeter wall and a mesh vertex, not an opening-to-cell pass.
2. **Bounce-out after entering the mouth (HIGH).** Nothing checks that a ball which enters an opening, then rebounds out, is not counted. `onCapture` fires on first entry and no test checks a later exit.
3. **Scoring from behind or below (HIGH, partly covered).** shotaudit.test.mjs:76 checks only that a ball was seen on the front side at some point. It uses 150 random shots with a floor of `n >= 3`. No deterministic test covers a path that enters through the hive shell into a cell.
4. **Solver and forward-shot NaN or Infinity inputs (MED-HIGH).** None of the 8 files feeds NaN or Infinity into `Core.solve`, `evaluateShot`, `forwardShot`, or `ShooterPlant.step`. The only NaN case is `pickEquivalentTurretAngle(NaN, ...)` in aimcontrol.test.mjs:48. Behaviour under NaN is unverified.
5. **Moving shots, limited scope (MED).** shoot_moving.test.mjs only covers lateral motion along the opening plane, `om` fixed at 0, a fixed pose, 20 shots per run, and one aim cell per hive. Not covered: forward and back motion, rotation (`om` nonzero), accel, and fireHeld while the robot moves.
6. **Both kinds through the full path (MED).** Kind-specific launch and single shots are covered (ballkinds_shoot.test.mjs). Not covered: a nectar hopper shot in shootend2end style, a mixed 4-ball hold fired into the hive, or nectar scoring while moving.
7. **Turret wrap (MED).** `pickEquivalentTurretAngle` and the `modeCommand` 190 to -170 case are tested. Not covered: a ShooterPlant rate-limited sweep across the +/-180 seam, the solver with robot heading crossing +/-pi, and any e2e shot near the seam.
8. **Hold limit 4 (LOW).** Covered by intake guard (3), the mixed-hold cap in ballkinds_shootfix, and the e2e hopper reload. Missing: a held-full robot that drives over a free ball should not load it. Low risk.
9. **Tip weight (LOW, stale comments).** shotrules and ballkinds_shootfix comments cite nectar 1.654. The model `web/js/hivetip_model.js` uses `nectarWeight: 1.75` (INFERRED). The assertions (for example 4p+2n false, 4p+3n true, 0p+4n false) pass under both values, so they do not separate them. Test comments should be corrected, and a case that separates 1.654 from 1.75 would help.
10. **Weak floors (LOW).** shotaudit fuzz asserts `n >= 3` and checks only `scored`/`free` state. The `stuck ball` test checks only the cell sensor.

## Not tested at all (from the 8 files)

- Bounce-out: none.
- Tunnelling at hive speeds: failing (t12).
- Moving shots with rotation: none.
- NaN or Infinity inputs to the shot path: none.
- Turret wrap through the solver and plant: none beyond angle picking.
