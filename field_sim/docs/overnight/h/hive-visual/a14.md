## Why only one sample in 10 s
- rAF measured at about 3 frames in 5 s (about 0.6 fps; `fps.mjs`, run in a visible page).
- `app.js:1077` clamps dt to 0.05 s per frame. `hivecycle.js:60` sets `animationDuration: 0.6` s. Smoothstep reaches the target after 12 frames, which is about 20 s of wall time at 0.6 fps.
- So the tip animation is frame-driven, and this headless setup cannot show it in real time. This is a limit of the test rig, not a measured sim defect. On a real GPU at 60 fps the same move takes about 0.6 s.

## Consistency check
- rotX matched `rad(angleDeg - (-30))` in every sample pair: -28.82 deg gives 0.0206 rad, -25.56 deg gives 0.0776 rad. The -30 is the `audience_up` stable angle.
- Settled `far_up` at 30 deg gives rotX 1.0472 rad, which is 60 deg, i.e. `30 - (-30)`. Consistent.
- The state label switches to `far_up` only at settle (`hivecycle.js` `_settleAt`). Mid-move it still reads `audience_up`.
