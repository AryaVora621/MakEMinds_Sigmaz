## Coverage gaps
1. **Visible tip angle over time.** The model angle is checked. No test compares the rendered hive pose (three.js mesh rotation) with `HiveDynamics.angleDeg` frame by frame. hivetip.test.mjs checks physics-hive angle equals shown angle only at the end of the move.
2. **Balls exiting an opening after a tip.** Only entry and capture are tested. No test sends a ball out through an opening after a tip, or checks balls leaving the now-down cell when the hive flips. `releaseOnTip` is checked in the model only (hivecycle.test.mjs), not with physics.
3. **Mirrored red/blue hive.** Red and blue are looped in hivetip and physics3d, but nothing checks that blue geometry (opening, aim point, up/down cells) is the mirror of red's, or that the blue angle sign is right. The only sign check is `a0 < 0 ? 'far_up' : 'audience_up'` in hivetip.test.mjs.
4. **Damper contact.** hivecycle.js line 16 comments on a damper touching the frame in the up cell. No test references a damper, its frame contact, or its effect on the swing.
5. **Pile load during swing.** Many balls rolling in the cell while the hive moves are not tested. Only huge-dt and determinism cases exist.
6. **h_hive-physics_* and h_flower-mech_* files.** Outside the glob and not run or read in this pass.
