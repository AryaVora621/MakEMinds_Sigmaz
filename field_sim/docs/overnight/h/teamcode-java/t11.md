## What the official rules say (fetched)
- Rule SG9 (Match Loads), fetched from https://events.vex.com/storage/game_manual/VEX_V5_Robotics_Competition_2025-2026_Push_Back/rules/SG9.html
  - Drive Team Members place Match Load Blocks "into either of the Loaders adjacent to their Alliance Station".
  - Timing (SG9 text): "Blocks may only be added to Loaders during the Driver Controlled Period of a Match."
  - A Block can only be added "if there are no Blocks partially or entirely within the orange portion of that Loader."
  - Q&A 2949 (linked from events.vex.com QA/2949, seen in search only, not fetched): drive team "can hold as many Blocks as they like."
- Rule VUG3, fetched from https://events.vex.com/storage/game_manual/VEX_V5_Robotics_Competition_2025-2026_Push_Back/rules/VUG3.html
  - Quoted: Drive Team Members may add Match Load Blocks to the Loaders adjacent to their Alliance Station "during the Autonomous Period and Driver Controlled Period of the Match."
- CONFLICT: SG9 says driver-controlled only; VUG3 says autonomous and driver-controlled. Which is current is UNVERIFIED. Check the latest manual PDF (WebSearch found a manual at content.vexrobotics.com, PushBack2.1.pdf, not fetched). Do not design around either until resolved.
- Loader definition: not found on either fetched page. UNVERIFIED.
- Manual versions seen in search snippets only: 2.0 (Sept 4, 2025) and 2.1 (Oct 9, 2025). Not fetched.

## FTC flower as an analogy (ANALOGY ONLY)
This is an analogy only. VEX Push Back is not FTC, and no source connects the two. The "flower" is this project's name for a petal-style push feeder (keyboard G in the sim). Nothing below is a verified FTC or VEX fact.
1. Model the flower as N petal meshes around a hub, each a child of a pivot Object3D at the hub.
2. Each petal has a closed angle and an open angle. Animate the angle with a first-order approach (lerp per tick), not an instant snap.
3. The hub position is a constant in the sim config. Mark its value TODO until measured. Do not invent it.
4. Keep the petal count and the sweep angle as named config constants, both TODO.
5. Only the petal in the sweep arc pushes a ball. Test the arc with the ball-center distance to the hub.
6. A ball is pushed when its center is inside the petal sweep radius and the petal is moving toward the exit.
7. Pushed balls move with a fixed exit speed in the petal direction, speed TODO.
8. Use the existing ball hold model; do not add a second ball counter. Respect holdLimit 4.
9. Do not use Pedro chain callbacks for the flower state (they never fire).
10. Expose flower state (open or closed, cycle count) through the same telemetry path as the other mechanisms.
11. Add a JS test: a ball at the hub pushes out after one full cycle, a ball outside the arc does not move.
12. Add a JS test: the petal angle stays within [closed, open] for 1000 ticks.
13. Visual check: headless Chrome screenshot of the flower mid-sweep, saved to the scratchpad dir.
14. Note in the sim that the flower is an analogy and that its geometry is unverified.
