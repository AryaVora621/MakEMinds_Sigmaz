## Field_sim exporters (generated Pedro Java)

  - Lines 301-308 (`run${n}` follow state): markers are polled with `getChainIndex()` and `getCurrentTValue()` (305-306), then flushed at path end (307), then `isBusyOrTimeout()` (308).

- `field_sim/web/js/pathing.js` `toJava` also does NOT emit callbacks.
